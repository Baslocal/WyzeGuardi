ℹ️ About Device Management

Online Status: A device is considered "online" if it has sent a heartbeat within the last {{ home_threshold }} minutes (your HOME threshold).

Presence Detection: When any enabled online device exists, you're considered HOME and cameras will turn OFF.

Disabling Devices: Disabled devices are ignored for presence detection.
